## Key facts

- **System:** JAMA, ISBT 049, ratified at ISBT Kuala Lumpur 2026
- **Antigen:** JIMI (JAMA1), high prevalence
- **Gene / protein:** *F11R* / JAM-A (two extracellular Ig domains, D1 and D2)
- **JIMI-negative genotype:** homozygous *F11R* c.644G>A, p.Arg215Gln (exon 6, D2)
- **Shown:** the antibody binds Arg215 JAM-A on red cells, and binds JAM-A on platelets in vitro
- **Not yet known:** clinical importance, including transfusion reactions, HDFN risk beyond one family, FNAIT and platelet refractoriness
